Tower Tap: World Heights · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Tower Tap: World Heights ("the game") is made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited, Rajkot, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game handles on iOS, iPadOS, tvOS, Android and Chrome.

What we collect ourselves

Without signing in: nothing. Your progress (coins, upgrades, stars, best height, settings) and your in-game profile (name, avatar and frame) are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

Optional sign-in and cloud save (iOS and Android, from version 1.2.0). If you choose to sign in with Apple or Facebook in Settings › Account, we store a copy of your game save (the same progress and profile listed above) in Google Firebase, linked to an account ID, so you can continue on another phone or tablet. Google Firebase also keeps the sign-in details the provider passes on with the account: for Sign in with Apple, the email address you choose to share (usually an Apple private relay address) and your name if you share it; for Facebook, your public profile name. We use them only to run your account, never show them to other players, and never use them for ads or tracking. We do not receive your friends list, contacts or password. Signing in is never required to play, and it is not offered to players the store reports as under 13.

  • Sign in with Apple: Apple gives us an account ID for this app and, if you allow it, your name and an email address (you can choose Hide My Email).
  • Facebook: we use Facebook Login only, with Limited Login on iOS, and request only your public profile (ID and name). Facebook app events and advertising ID collection are switched off in the game.
  • Your cloud save is updated after each build and kept until you delete your account. Settings › Account › Delete account removes the cloud save and the account straight away and, for Apple, revokes the game's access to your Apple account.

Game statistics (iOS and Android, from version 1.3.0; rules updated in 1.6.1). To learn which parts of the game players enjoy and whether they come back, the game can send anonymous gameplay counts to Google Analytics for Firebase: which modes and levels you play, how builds end, which tips you see, coins earned and spent, awards earned, settings changed, ads offered and shown, purchases completed (the product, never an amount from us), your device type, OS version and country (from your IP address, which Google does not keep). They use a random app instance ID on your device, are never linked to your name, your account or your advertising ID, are not used for ads or to build a profile, and are deleted after 2 months.

  • In the EEA, UK, Switzerland, India, Canada, Brazil and other places where a consent form is required, statistics stay off until you turn them on after a short notice. Players there who had statistics on before version 1.6.1 are asked again.
  • Elsewhere they are on, with a one-line notice when you first reach the home screen, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings › Game statistics with one tap.
  • They are never collected on Apple TV, Google TV or Chrome, from players under 18 (as reported by the App Store or Google Play, or by the year-of-birth question in India), or from players in India whose age is not known.
  • Your choice is kept only on your device, never in your game save or cloud copy, and is removed by Delete my data.
  • While statistics are on, Settings shows the first 8 characters of your statistics ID. Send it to us to ask Google to delete that data; Delete my data also resets the ID on your device.

India: you can read a Hindi summary of this policy at privacy-hi.html. For questions or complaints about your data, email support@encircletechnologies.com; you can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Sharing your tower (from version 1.3.0). The Share button makes a picture of your tower and its stats on your device and hands it to your phone's share sheet. We do not upload or keep the picture; what happens next is up to the app you share it with.

Reminders (iOS and Android, from version 1.4.0). If you turn on reminders (on the home screen or in Settings › Reminders), the game schedules notifications on your device for things like your free gift, daily bonus, supply crate and the Weekly Challenge. They are created and shown by your phone; nothing is sent to us or to any server. Turn them off in Settings › Reminders or in your phone's notification settings.

Cloud save in your own Apple or Google account (from 1.6.0)

The game saves your progress in your own account, so you keep it when you change or reset a device. On iPhone and iPad it is stored in your iCloud key-value storage, without your player name, and the Apple TV app shares your stars and best height through the same storage. On Android it is stored in your Google Play Games Saved Games, with a picture of your best tower, the time played and a short description. Apple or Google holds this data under their own policies. Encircle receives none of it and cannot read it.

To turn it off, use Settings › Cloud save. To delete it, use Settings › Delete my data. If you are not signed in to Play Games, or iCloud is off on this device, your cloud copy stays: sign in and use Delete my data again. Account sync, the optional sign-in with Apple or Facebook, is separate and is described above.

India: account sync is for adults. If you are under 18, or your age is unknown, the game uses only your own iCloud or Play Games account. If you tap account sync in India, the game asks your year of birth and keeps only whether you are 18 or over, never the year.

Your choices

  • On iOS you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in iOS Settings › Privacy › Tracking.
  • In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and where local law requires, you can give or withdraw consent for personalized ads in the game under Settings › Privacy choices.
  • In India, Canada and Brazil, and for players under 18, ads are not personalized unless an adult turns on Settings › Personalised ads (from 1.6.1). On iOS, Apple's tracking prompt is shown there only after that.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Remove Ads, a one-time purchase, turns off full-screen ads.
  • Settings › Game statistics turns anonymous game statistics on or off. Turning them off also resets the statistics ID on your device.
  • Settings › Cloud save turns the save in your own iCloud or Play Games account on or off.
  • Settings › Delete my data deletes your progress on the device, your cloud copy, your account sync if you use it and, on iOS, your Game Center awards. On Android a small marker with no progress in it stays in Play Games, so your other devices delete their copy too. If you are not signed in to Play Games, or iCloud is off on this device, your cloud copy stays: sign in and use Delete my data again. Remove Ads stays with your store account: use Restore Purchases to get it back. Coins are not restored.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes the game data on your device. It does not remove the copy in your iCloud or Play Games account.

Chrome extension

The Chrome version shows no ads, has no purchases and collects no data. Progress is stored in your browser only.

Apple TV

The Apple TV version shows no ads, has no purchases and collects no data. Your stars and best heights are stored on the Apple TV and synced between your Apple TVs through your own iCloud account (Apple iCloud key-value storage), which your iPhone and iPad also use from 1.6.0. Leaderboards use Apple Game Center.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

Where the law requires it, the game asks the App Store or Google Play for your age range when you first open it, after a significant update, before a purchase, and otherwise at most once a year. It uses the answer only to set child-safe ads and to ask a parent to approve significant updates. The game keeps only that setting and the date it asked, on your device; it never stores the age range itself and never sends it anywhere. Delete my data or uninstalling the game removes it.

Security and retention

Data handled by the services above is sent over encrypted connections and kept according to their policies. We do not sell personal information.
