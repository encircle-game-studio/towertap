Tower Tap: World Heights · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Tower Tap: World Heights ("the game") is made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited, Rajkot, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game handles on iOS, iPadOS, Android and Chrome.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The game has no account and no sign-up. Your progress (coins, upgrades, stars, best height, settings) and your in-game profile (name, avatar and frame) are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

Their policies: Google, RevenueCat, Apple.

Your choices

  • On iOS you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in iOS Settings › Privacy › Tracking.
  • In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and where local law requires, you can give or withdraw consent for personalized ads in the game under Settings › Privacy choices.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Remove Ads, a one-time purchase, turns off full-screen ads.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data on your device.

Chrome extension

The Chrome version shows no ads, has no purchases and collects no data. Progress is stored in your browser only.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

Security and retention

Data handled by the services above is sent over encrypted connections and kept according to their policies. We do not sell personal information.

Contact and requests

For questions or data requests, email support@encircletechnologies.com. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell players in the game.
