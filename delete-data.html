Delete your Tower Tap: World Heights data

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Tower Tap: World Heights is made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited. Unless you choose to sign in, your data lives only on your device. This page explains how to delete it and how to ask us to delete the rest.

If you signed in (cloud save)

Open the game, go to Settings › Account › Delete account and confirm. This deletes your cloud save and your Tower Tap account at once and, if you used Sign in with Apple, revokes the game's access to your Apple account. You can also choose to erase the progress on that device at the same time. If you no longer have the game installed, email us as in Step 2 and say that you used Sign in with Apple or Facebook; we will delete the account and cloud save within 30 days. Facebook users can also remove the game in Facebook › Settings › Apps and websites.

Step 1: delete the game data on your device

  • Android: uninstall Tower Tap. This deletes your progress, profile and settings. If Android backup is on, a copy of the save file may also be in your Google account backup; you can delete it in Google Drive › Backups or turn backup off in Android settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: delete the app. This deletes your progress, profile and settings from the device.
  • Chrome: remove the extension. Its progress is deleted with it.

Step 2: ask us to delete the data held for us

  1. Email support@encircletechnologies.com with the subject "Delete my Tower Tap data".
  2. Say which platform you play on (Android or iOS).
  3. If you bought anything, include the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt so we can find the matching purchase record.

We reply within 30 days and confirm when the deletion is done.

Step 3: advertising and leaderboard data

  • Ads: Google AdMob handles ad data. On Android, delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). On iOS, turn off tracking for the game in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Google's ad controls are at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Leaderboards: scores are kept by Google Play Games or Apple Game Center under your own account. Manage or delete them in the Play Games settings or in Game Center settings.
