Delete your Tower Tap: World Heights data

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Tower Tap: World Heights is made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited. Your data lives on your device and in your own iCloud or Play Games account, unless you choose to sign in. This page explains how to delete it and how to ask us to delete the rest.

Delete everything from inside the game

Open the game, go to Settings › Delete my data and confirm. This removes your progress on the device, your iCloud copy or Play Games save, your account sync if you use it and, on iOS, your Game Center awards. On Android, scores and achievements stay in your Play Games profile, where you can delete them. On Android a small marker with no progress in it stays in Play Games, so your other devices delete their copy too. Remove Ads stays with your store account: use Restore Purchases to get it back. Coins are not restored. The Apple TV app keeps its own copy until you delete the app there. If you are not signed in to Play Games, or iCloud is off on this device, your cloud copy stays: sign in and use Delete my data again.

Deleting the app does not remove your cloud copy. To remove it without the game: on iPhone or iPad, open iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage. On Android, Google's steps are: open play.google.com/games/profile in a browser, choose Your data, then Delete Play Games account & data, and under Delete individual game data choose Delete next to Tower Tap. Google's labels can differ by version; see Google's help page Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile.

If you signed in (account sync)

Open the game, go to Settings › Account sync › Delete account and confirm. This deletes your cloud save and your Tower Tap account at once and, if you used Sign in with Apple, revokes the game's access to your Apple account. If it shows an error, sign in again and retry, or email us and we delete it within 30 days. If you no longer have the game installed, email us as in Step 2 and say that you used Sign in with Apple or Facebook; we will delete the account and cloud save within 30 days. Facebook users can also remove the game in Facebook › Settings › Apps and websites.

Step 1: delete the game data on your device

  • Android: uninstall Tower Tap. This deletes your progress, profile and settings. If Android backup is on, a copy of the save file may also be in your Google account backup; you can delete it in Google Drive › Backups or turn backup off in Android settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: delete the app. This deletes your progress, profile and settings from the device.
  • Chrome: remove the extension. Its progress is deleted with it.

Step 2: ask us to delete the data held for us

  1. Email support@encircletechnologies.com with the subject "Delete my Tower Tap data".
  2. Say which platform you play on (Android or iOS).
  3. If you bought anything, include the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt so we can find the matching purchase record.

We reply within 30 days and confirm when the deletion is done.

Step 3: advertising and leaderboard data

  • Ads: Google AdMob handles ad data. On Android, delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). On iOS, turn off tracking for the game in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Google's ad controls are at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Leaderboards: scores are kept by Google Play Games or Apple Game Center under your own account. Manage or delete them in the Play Games settings or in Game Center settings.

Game statistics are anonymous and deleted automatically after 2 months. To stop them, turn off Settings › Game statistics, then use Settings › Delete my data (it also resets the statistics ID on your device). To have data already sent deleted sooner, email the statistics ID shown in Settings while statistics are on to support@encircletechnologies.com and we will ask Google to delete it.
